Tide & Lantern — a lighthouse keeper card duel. Three sea lanes, one ship each: push ships into your harbor with Waves, reverse the current with Lanterns, shove ships away with Squalls, shield a lane with an Anchor, or push every ship at once with a Gale. Dock a ship to score a point and damage the enemy harbor. First to 7 points, or a harbor at 0 HP, wins. Playable with mouse and keyboard, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click a card, then a lane — or press 1–5 to pick a card, arrow keys to pick a lane, Enter to play it. The Keeper (AI) answers every move with a readable plan: it finishes dockings, lights Lanterns when you are about to dock, Squalls the lane closest to your harbor, and Anchors ships in danger.
